# pahwq: Calculate site-specific water quality guidelines for PAHs

Calculate water quality guidlines for polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons
(PAHs) for a specific location, taking into account the phototoxicity of
specific PAHs at a given depth. Light absorption at depth is calculated
using the TUV model ('https://github.com/NCAR/TUV').
